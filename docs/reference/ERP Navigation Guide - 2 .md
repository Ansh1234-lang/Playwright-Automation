**Purchase Order (PO)**

 

**Operations**

 

1\. Create PO (Inward \> Manage PO \> Create PO) 

Purchase Order (PO) is created by the Purchase Executive.The items mentioned in the PO may arrive either in a single consignment or in multiple consignments.

2\. Search PO number (Inward \> Manage Vehicle \> Manage Vehicle Entry)

Loaded truck , carrying the purchased material, arrives at the mill gate. Security Guard searches the Purchase order number.

3\. Add Vehicle Details (Inward \> Manage Vehicle \>Manage Vehicle Entry)

Afterwards, Security Guard does entry of no. of bags of particular varieties of paddy present in truck or trucks.

4\. Assign sample to Lab Supervisor ( Inward \> Manage Vehicle \> Vehicle Items Details)

Once the Security Guard does the entry on software , vehicle details starts reflecting to Security Supervisor. Samples of the different / single variety are assigned to the Lab Supervisor by Security Supervisor for further testing and analysis.

5\. Assign sample to Lab Executive (Quality Check \> Inward Quality Check \> Manage Lab Test)

Lab Supervisor assigns the samples to the Lab Executive for testing and analysis.

6\. Accepts sample (Quality Check \> Inward Quality Check \> Manage Lab Test)

Lab Executive accepts or rejects the sample on the basis of his ability to test the sample at that particular moment.

If accepts then sample ID is highlighted as yellow in colour and the lab fields are entered. 

If rejects the sample then the step at “Assign sample to Lab Executive” becomes null and void. The sample is available to be assigned to Lab Executive again.

7\. Create Sample Input (Quality Check \> Inward Quality Check \> Manage Lab Test)

Lab Executive records the measured values of the defined quality parameters by creating the sample input.

8\. Approve Lab Report (Quality Check \> Inward Quality Check\>Lab Reports)

Lab Supervisor can approve the sample.

If the sample is rejected , an option to resample appears on screen. If resample button is clicked , new sample ID for the same sample is created for resampling. After entering the lab field values , this resample can only be either rejected or can be sent for review.

Note : If there is confusion, the Lab Supervisor will put the sample under review. This sample will be reviewed with application of cut (if approved)by Quality manager and this cut will then reflect in invoice. The quality manager can reject the sample at this step as well.

If the sample is rejected n no. of times then resample button also appears n no. of times after rejection. There is no restriction.

9\. Approve Truck Entry (Inward \> Manage Vehicle \> Vehicle Items Details)

Based on the approved laboratory results, the truck entry is approved, and the vehicle is permitted to proceed inside the mill premises by Security Supervisor. The Security Guard looks at the status updated by Security Supervisor and opens the gate.

If the truck entry is rejected , the details of truck doesn’t get reflected in “Add measured weight”.

10\. Add invoice details with weight (Weight measurement\> Inward Weight Measure\>Add measured weight)

Now , the truck reaches the weight bridge, where invoice details (invoice issued by supplier) and the gross weight of the loaded vehicle are entered by Weight Executive. 

11\. Assign Warehouse and Warehouse Supervisor (Weight measurement\> Inward Weight Measure\>Manage weight jobs)

Warehouse Manager gets to know about the arrival of vehicle and then assigns the designated warehouse and Store Manager.

12\. Assign Partition, Stack , Warehouse Executive (Truck\> Inward Unloading\> Manage items)

Store Manager assigns the partition, stack, and Store Keeper for accommodating the product in warehouse.

13\. Unload items (Truck\> Inward Unloading\> Unload items)

Store Keeper unloads the material into the assigned location in the warehouse. Only after this entry is made , then only row of Job in “Add measured weight” appears.

14\. Add Tare weight (Weight measurement\> Inward Weight Measure \> Add measured weight)

After unloading, the empty truck is again weighed at the weight bridge, and the tare weight is recorded by Weight Executive.

Only after this entry, Invoice gets activated and can be clicked.

**Accounts**

1\.	Create invoice (Finance \> Accounting \>Purchase order\> Purchase account)

     Upon completion of the unloading and weight measurement activities, the purchase invoice is generated by Accountant.

2\.	See invoice details (Finance\> Accounting \>Purchase order\> Purchase invoices report)

    The details of the generated invoice can subsequently be viewed in the Purchase Invoice Reports. The access to see this is given to Accountant.

  **Operations**

15\. Click on green Exit button ( Inward\> Manage Vehicle\>Vehicle Items Details)

     Exit button turns from grey colour to green colour indicating that the truck can be exited as invoice has been generated. Now, the Guard Supervisor clicks on the green coloured Exit button and lets the truck go out of the gate.
